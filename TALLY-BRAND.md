# TALLY — brand one-pager (draft for the owner to edit)

## The one truth
**We keep count.** A tally is how working people have always counted jobs done — a stroke
for every one, a strike through four to bank five. Tally builds websites and search for
local businesses, and *counts what they bring in*: every enquiry, every call, every booking
gets a mark. If it can't be counted, we don't claim it.

Everything — logo, copy, case studies, reporting — must serve that sentence.

## Why it's ours
- The site's case studies are already tallies: 16 enquiries. 1,818 passengers. £10,000 from zero.
- The tick-strip `||||||||||` has been in the page furniture since the first draft — the brand
  was in the design before the name arrived.
- The audience (trades, local firms) respects counting: day rates, job sheets, invoices.
  No agency poetry. Marks on a board.

## Voice
Plain, specific, counted. Numbers before adjectives. Never "digital solutions" — say
"the site brought in 16 enquiries in March". British, direct, quietly confident.
Banned: pricing on the site, fluff verbs (elevate, unlock, empower), fake urgency.

## System (already live on the page)
- **Ground:** ink `#0B0B0D` / `#101114` · **Type ground:** cream `#F4F1EA`
- **Client colours stay the clients':** taxi gold `#C9A961`, JDH blue `#4C79F0`, Deen green `#4CA24F`
- **Display:** Anton, tight leading, edge-to-edge · **Text:** system sans
- **Motif:** the tally stroke. Tick-strips as separators; the five-bar gate as the icon;
  strokes = jobs counted. Never decorative squiggles — every stroke means a counted thing.

## Logo directions (audition with the LOGO switcher, bottom-left)
1. **® wordmark** — Anton "Tally®", tick-strip carries the motif. Quietest.
2. **Five-bar gate** — 4 strokes + strike icon before the wordmark. The "counted" mark;
   works as favicon/stamp alone.
3. **Struck LL** — the wordmark's own LLs become the tally, one strike through both.
   The name performs its meaning. Boldest; must stay legible small.
4. **Gate cap** — wordmark with a superscript mini-gate where ® would sit: "counted,
   certified". Subtle, invoice-friendly.

## Small-size duty (a mark must survive all of these)
16px favicon · 18px nav · email signature · invoice header · one-colour on dark van panel.
