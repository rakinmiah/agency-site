# THRUM — brand one-pager (draft; supersedes TALLY-BRAND.md if THRUM wins)

## The one truth
**Thrum is the sound of a business that's working.** Phones going, van out, bookings
landing — a steady hum, not a one-off bang. We build the sites that keep it going.
Second lineage: on a loom, thrums are the thread-ends where the cloth was cut free —
the weaving root (wyrd's family) that started this naming trail.

## The enemy
The silent website. Pretty, expensive, sits there. All positioning is against silence:
"Before: silence. After: 16 enquiries a month."

## Voice
Plain, specific, counted (unchanged from the page). The sound metaphor lives in
artefacts and headlines, never forced into the owner's own email voice.
Radio answer, rehearsed once: "Thrum — like drum, with a T-H."

## System (already live on the page)
Ink #0B0B0D / #101114 · cream #F4F1EA · client colours stay the clients' ·
Anton display + system sans · quiet motion only.

## Logo directions (?logo= switcher, bottom-left)
1. **Hum line** — one soft oscillation in a horizontal rule. Latent everywhere already:
   CTA underlines, case-card progress bars, section rules. Favicon = the wave alone.
2. **Thrums** — a hanging bundle of uneven strokes (thread-ends). The existing
   |||||||||| tick-strip re-read with a deeper story. Secondary texture candidate.
3. **Living mark** — the giant wordmark itself hums: a barely-perceptible slow
   oscillation (reduced-motion: static). Display-scale identity; small sizes stay plain.

Likely final system: hum line as working mark + thrums as texture + plain Anton wordmark.

## Applications
- Monthly client report titled **"The Thrum"** — what came in this month.
- Pitch line: "Hear that? That's your site working."
- Case studies: silence → thrum framing.
