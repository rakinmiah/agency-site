one thread, drawn as one curve
The oo is real lemniscate geometry now — a continuous path with a clean crossing, cut to Jost's stem weight and set on the x-height for lowercase, the cap-height for uppercase. Every design is shown both ways: lowercase for signature and small use, and UPPERCASE with every letter one size for the hero title.
