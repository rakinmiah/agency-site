WARP, WEFT, AND THE ESSENTIAL TOOL
Old English gelōma: the tool. The warp is fixed to the frame once and holds — the website. The weft runs through it continuously and is what actually makes cloth — search, ads, care. Every candidate below makes one of those two ideas visible, and nothing else.
